grb2-invented-labels.csv: labels INVENTED for MaveScape's tests of control sets (wave 8, slice 1),
not classifications of any variant. Each row plants a case the reader of a table of labels must
handle: P62993's numbering (the GRB2 SH3 example's target is its residues 159-214), the words of
each side (pathogenic, LP, abnormal; benign, likely benign, normal, B), protein changes written as
MAVE-HGVS, one-letter or ClinVar's titles, a change labeled on both sides, a change labeled twice on
one side, labels not read (VUS, uncertain significance, empty), a position outside the target, a
residue the target does not have, and a name that is no variant. validation/run.mjs, suite
controls, says what each must become.
