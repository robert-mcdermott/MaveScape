Reading the map

GRB2's map colored by score: in this abundance assay blue is less function, red more, white like the wild type.
  • Scored cells are colored by score on a diverging scale centered on the wild type and symmetric about it. Low scores are blue and high scores red. Which end is loss of function depends on the assay, so the legend names the ends from the readout ("less function", "more function") when the design states its direction, and says only "lower" and "higher" otherwise (What the assay measures).
  • Low confidence cells (fewer replicates than expected, or a zero count) are drawn paler, with a corner mark.
  • Filtered cells are hatched; missing cells (not in the table) are gray with a dot; cells outside the designed tiles are left empty. None of them is ever drawn as "no effect": each is at least ΔE 10 from the neutral color in both themes and both palettes.
  • The reference residue at each position is outlined.

Above the map, the median score of each position; at the right, of each substitution. Positions are numbered on the target and, with an offset, on the reference. The overview strip below shows where the view is.

Settings

  • Color by score, standard error, replicates used, input count (log), or for a run that compares conditions the differential score: each condition against the reference, centered on no difference, a variant with no difference shown filtered with why (Scoring).
  • Rows in biochemical order (charged, polar, aliphatic, aromatic, special, stop), by hydrophobicity or alphabetically.
  • Colors: blue–red or purple–orange.
  • Zoom with ⌘ or Ctrl and the wheel, or the buttons; drag to pan; Fit shows the whole map.
Colored by standard error, the uncertain cells stand out: in the simulated example, the variants rare in the library. Colored by the differential score: the binding site's variants lose fitness only with the ligand. The inspector gives the variant's score in each condition and the difference.

Selections

Click a cell to select it and see it in the inspector; ⌘-click adds to the selection; ⇧-drag selects a rectangle. Save as… keeps the selection by name in the workspace, listed in the dataset tree and under the map; Export writes the selected variants with their scores (CSV) or the selection itself (JSON).

Positions 35–37 selected and saved as a named selection.

The variant inspector

For a variant, the inspector shows its name as written and in MAVE-HGVS, its class and position; its score with standard error and 95% interval, or why it has none (the filter stage that left it out); its flags; each replicate's counts before and after selection, score and whether it was used; with conditions compared, its score in each and each difference with its interval and q (paired, each pair's difference too); every sample's counts; the other substitutions at its position against the run's range; the sequence around it; and the run it comes from.

Export

Export writes the map as SVG (vector, for figures) or PNG at three times the resolution, in the export theme, with its legend.

In words and by keyboard

Below the map, In words describes it: the cells in each state, the least and most tolerant positions, the scale. Every cell is also in a table. With the map focused, the arrow keys move between cells, each announced, and Enter selects. Color-vision-friendly colors are a setting in the appearance menu.
